How to Book TPC Scottsdale's Stadium Course

Updated for the 2026 season

The Stadium Course is the one place the rest of us get to walk the same holes as the Phoenix Open — the 16th-hole stadium green included. It's a true public booking, no membership gate, which makes it one of the most in-demand tee sheets in Arizona golf, especially in the months surrounding the tournament.

The booking window

Public tee times open on a rolling 7–30 day window through the official site, tpc.com/scottsdale, or through third-party platforms like GolfNow. During peak season — roughly January through April — plan to book as close to the full 30 days out as you can manage; prime morning and weekend times during that stretch disappear quickly.

Pricing isn't fixed

TPC Scottsdale runs fully dynamic pricing on the Stadium Course — the green fee shifts with season, day of week, and even time of day. A Tuesday afternoon in July and a Saturday morning in February are not remotely the same price, and the gap can be significant. If budget matters as much as timing, it's worth checking rates across a few different days before locking in a date.

If you're a TPC Pass holder

Pass holders book differently — a shorter 3-day window applies across both TPC Scottsdale courses, rather than the public 7–30 day range.

Practical takeaway: because pricing floats with demand, the best strategy for visitors isn't always "book the earliest available slot" — it's "watch for a good time at a good price," which usually means checking in regularly rather than grabbing the first thing you see at day 30.
