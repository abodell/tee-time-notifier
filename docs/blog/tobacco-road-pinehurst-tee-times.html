We Beat Tobacco Road's Own Waitlist to a Tee Time

A real trip, a few weeks ago

A few buddies and I took a trip down to the Pinehurst/Southern Pines area a few weeks back. Mid Pines, Pine Needles, Tot Hill Farm were all on the short list, but the course the whole trip got built around was Tobacco Road — if you've played it, you already know why; if you haven't, you've probably at least heard it's one of the weirdest, most love-it-or-hate-it, fully-booked courses in American golf.

Tobacco Road was sold out for the dates we wanted. The course runs its own official waitlist through a platform called Noteefy — you put in your dates, group size, and preferred times, and it texts you if a cancellation opens one up. It's a legitimately solid system, and it's the one the course itself tells you to use.

I signed up for Noteefy like anyone would. I also had TeeSignal — my own app — running an alert for the exact same dates and group size, watching the same public tee sheet.

TeeSignal got there first. Twice.

Twice on that trip, TeeSignal's text landed before Noteefy's did. Not by much — a few minutes each time — but at Tobacco Road, a few minutes is the whole ballgame. We ended up with two tee times there because of it. We also used TeeSignal to land our Southern Pines round on the same trip, no drama, just an alert that fired and a booking that went through.

I'm not trying to trash Noteefy here — it's a good tool and a lot of courses lean on it well. The honest takeaway is simpler than "mine is better": when two systems are watching the exact same public availability, whichever one checks faster wins, and that's the golfer who gets the time. That's just how polling works, regardless of whose logo is on it.

Why this matters past Tobacco Road

Everything in that corridor gets tight during trip season — Mid Pines, Pine Needles, Southern Pines, Tot Hill Farm, all of it. I haven't personally run every one of those down to a cancellation the way I did at Tobacco Road, but the logic doesn't change: set alerts for every course on your itinerary before you leave, and let something else do the refreshing instead of doing it yourself from a hotel room at 1am.
